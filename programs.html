Pillar 1 • SDG 5 (Gender Equality)

Women’s Institutions, Empowerment & Leadership

In rural Rajasthan, women historically face severe societal restrictions. Male dominance and patriarchal traditions have created barriers to education, mobility, leadership, and property ownership.

GMVS firmly believes that rural women are not merely beneficiaries, but the primary architects of community transformation. Our intervention organizes women into Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Village Organizations (VOs), and cluster-level federations.

Through regular meetings, monthly savings, internal lending, and bank linkages, women gain financial independence from local moneylenders. Building on this base, GMVS introduces vocational skill training in apparel tailoring, machine stitching, hand embroidery, and handicraft production.

800+ Active Self-Help Groups (SHGs) Over 45,000 women mobilized into self-governing village savings collectives.
Pioneering Cluster Development (Darji Online) Recognized by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in Mann Ki Baat (Ep. 92) for connecting rural women tailors to national and international markets.
Social Leadership & Panchayati Raj Participation Training SHG leaders to contest Panchayat elections, demand village infrastructure, and lead community development.

Pillar 2 • SDG 4 (Quality Education)

Child Rights, Protection & Girl Child Education

GMVS has been striving relentlessly for the protection and development of rural children since its inception in 1998.

In drought-hit agricultural belts, extreme poverty often compels parents to withdraw adolescent children from schools to work in crop fields or migrate as child labourers. Girl children suffer disproportionately due to the deep-rooted cultural preference for boys' education and early marriage traditions.

GMVS teams conduct regular door-to-door village tracking, identifying dropouts and counselling parents. We establish community learning centres and village libraries where children receive free bridge coaching, textbooks, stationery, and nutrition support to smoothly transition back into formal government schools.

12,500+ Out-of-School Children Re-Enrolled Systematic campaigns eliminating child labour across Ajmer and Nagaur districts.
Adolescent Girl Forums (Kishori Mandals) Empowering adolescent girls with legal awareness, menstrual hygiene education, and resistance against child marriage.
Community Libraries & Digital Learning Labs Providing quiet learning environments and computer literacy to rural youth in remote Gram Panchayats.

Pillar 3 • SDG 6 & 13 (Water Security & Climate Action)

Natural Resource Management & Jal Shakti Mission

The problem of drought in Rajasthan has worsened over the past three decades due to severe ecological disturbance and the neglect of historic indigenous water conservation systems.

Historic techniques of rainwater conservation (Taankas, Khadins, Choukas, Talabs, and Nadi recharge) were overlooked, causing deep tubewells to run dry and soil to erode.

GMVS mobilizes entire villages to revive their traditional water commons. We undertake scientific watershed development, construct community rainwater recharge structures, repair earthen check dams, and implement ridge-to-valley soil moisture conservation.

180+ Water Harvesting Structures Revived & Built Securing drinking water for over 35,000 rural families during peak summer months.
50,000+ Native Tree Saplings Planted Plantation of drought-resilient native species (Khejri, Neem, Ber) on village pasturelands (Gauchar).
Water User Committees (Pani Samitis) Handing long-term ownership and maintenance of water bodies to local village women collectives.

Pillar 4 • SDG 3 (Good Health & Well-Being)

Health Concerns, Nutrition & Mother-Child Welfare

The health status of rural women and infants remains precarious due to severe geographical isolation, lack of transport, low awareness, anemia, and superstitious practices.

GMVS acts as a vital bridge between remote communities and public health systems. We organize regular village health and nutrition days (VHND), conduct hemoglobin screenings, monitor maternal malnutrition, and run comprehensive immunization campaigns for pregnant mothers and newborns.

In addition, GMVS has partnered with the National AIDS Control Organisation (NACO) and the Rajasthan State AIDS Control Society (RSACS) to conduct targeted interventions, peer counselling, STI clinic referrals, and stigma-reduction workshops in high-risk migration corridors.

65,000+ Health Screenings & Checkups Mobile medical camps reaching interior hamlets without primary healthcare access.
Menstrual Hygiene & Sanitary Pad Distribution Workshops in village high schools breaking taboos and providing affordable hygiene products.
NACO & RSACS Targeted Interventions Long-term preventive health and HIV awareness partnerships spanning high-risk transit zones.

Pillar 5 • SDG 1 & 8 (No Poverty & Decent Work)

Community Development, Agriculture & Sustainable Livelihoods

GMVS believes in holistic, decentralized development ensuring a dignified minimum standard of living for all rural families.

In a drought-prone state like Rajasthan, reliance on erratic monsoons causes persistent poverty. GMVS helps smallholder farmers adopt water-saving drip and sprinkler irrigation, introduces organic compost (vermicompost), distributes certified drought-hardy seed varieties, and provides veterinary support for livestock.

For landless women and marginalized families, GMVS provides micro-capital for goat rearing—a resilient source of daily income that requires minimal water and provides nutrition security through goat milk.

Goat Rearing Collectives for Landless Families Over 3,500 rural households lifted out of extreme poverty through livestock assets.
Climate-Smart Agriculture & Organic Manure Farmer field schools demonstrating non-pesticide pest management and soil carbon enrichment.
MNREGA Social Audits & Entitlement Awareness Ensuring 100 days of guaranteed wage employment for daily-wage labourers without corruption.
