Donor Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about making contributions to GMVS and tracking your impact.

How will my contribution be acknowledged?

You will receive an official acknowledgment receipt and letter of appreciation from GMVS within 2 to 3 working days once your contribution is confirmed by our accounts team.

Can Indian corporations partner with GMVS under CSR?

Yes. GMVS is registered under the Rajasthan Societies Registration Act, verified on NITI Aayog’s NGO Darpan portal, and eligible for corporate CSR partnerships. Please connect via our CSR portal or reach out directly to ed@gmvs.org.in.

Can foreign nationals or NRIs donate?

Yes. GMVS holds a valid FCRA registration (# 125410040) from the Ministry of Home Affairs. International remittances must be wired directly to the designated State Bank of India New Delhi Main Branch account listed above.

How is my donation utilized?

Over 88% of all funds directly reach grassroots project interventions — school supplies, livelihood kits, water harvesting structures, and free medical screenings. Administrative overhead is maintained strictly below 12%, and our accounts are audited annually by independent Chartered Accountants.
