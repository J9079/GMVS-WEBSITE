Partners & Collaborations

Catalyzing long-term, scalable grassroots transformation in partnership with national banks, corporate CSR foundations, and global development agencies.

The GMVS Advantage

Why Corporate CSR & Foundations Partner with GMVS

A proven 25-year institutional track record combining rigorous financial compliance with deep community trust.

Full Statutory Compliance

Registered under Section 135 Companies Act (CSR-1 eligible), compliant with Rajasthan Societies Registration Act & FCRA, and verified on NITI Aayog’s NGO Darpan portal.

FCRA Registered for Global Grants

Holding FCRA Registration No. 125410040 from the Ministry of Home Affairs, permitting international philanthropic grants and multinational CSR contributions.

Grassroots Execution Capacity

Permanent field presence across 2,500+ villages, supported by 150+ grassroots animators, trained women leaders, and direct access to remote Panchayats.

Rigorous M&E & Impact Audits

Quarterly progress milestones, geotagged asset verification for water structures, photographic documentation, and third-party social audits.
