About GMVS

Our Journey, Mission & Philosophy

Dedicated to empowering rural women, protecting child rights, securing drinking water, and restoring ecological harmony in Rajasthan since 1998.

Context & Origin

The Reality of Rural Rajasthan & Why GMVS Exists

Like many districts of Rajasthan, Ajmer district is severely rain-deficient. For decades, rainfall has steadily declined while groundwater tables plunge to critical depths.

Nature, cattle, and rural families have been affected in the worst possible form. Agriculture and animal husbandry are the lifeblood of rural communities; without water, crops fail and livestock perished. This triggered distress migration, breaking family structures, forcing women into extreme hardship, and pushing young children into hazardous child labour.

Entrenched gender discrimination, female illiteracy, child marriages, and social exclusion further barred rural women from decision-making or community governance.

With a determination to confront these deep-seated challenges, Gramin Mahila Vikas Sansthan (GMVS) was established in 1998. The founders walked village to village, knocking on doors in Bubani, Bhinai, and Srinagar blocks, identifying out-of-school children, listening to women's struggles, and building collective courage from within.

Core Intent

Vision, Mission & Strategic Objectives

Guiding our daily field operations across 2,500+ villages in Rajasthan.

Our Vision

“To make available sustainable alternatives of education, health, environment, clean drinking water, and dignified employment in rural social life.”

Our Mission

“To carry out women empowerment through self-help institutions, ensure children's right to quality education, provide preventive healthcare & immunization, and conserve natural water and vegetation resources.”

Institutional Objectives

Clean Drinking Water Access Assist problem villages in constructing and maintaining rainwater harvesting tanks and ponds.
Mother & Child Health Welfare Conduct village-level preventive healthcare, immunization drives, and adolescent health camps.
Women’s Self-Reliance & Skills Inculcate leadership in rural women and impart training in self-employment, tailoring, and micro-enterprises.
Modern Agri-Technology & Soil Health Introduce modern farming technology, water-saving micro-irrigation, and soil conservation techniques.
Eradication of Child Labour Run campaigns against child labour, re-enroll school dropouts, and establish community reading rooms.
Livestock & Animal Husbandry Support Motivate landless families for goat rearing, provide veterinary care, and enhance dairy productivity.
MNREGA Social Accountability Make information on MNREGA entitlements accessible to daily-wage workers and assist in job card issuance.
Handicrafts & Khadi Cluster Promotion Cooperate in promoting rural handicrafts, linking artisans with credit institutions and national markets.
Guiding Beliefs

Our Central Philosophy

GMVS is committed to respecting and recognizing indigenous knowledge as well as supporting local initiatives of the rural poor.

All programs at GMVS are built on a collective approach and a process of continual learning and reflection. They revolve around two fundamental tenets:

1. The right of every citizen to participate in the process of development.

2. The right of every human being to live in peace, dignity, and self-respect.

The greatest resource of GMVS is the community of women and men who have learned through their own lived experience that change is possible through the strength of collective effort and trust, and that lasting progress comes from within without undue external dependency.
