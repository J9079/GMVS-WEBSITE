Grassroots Recognition

Distinguished Honours & Felicitations

Acknowledged by district authorities, state leadership, and national platforms for 28 years of dedicated rural service.

District Honour • Ajmer
Office of District Collector, Ajmer

District Level Honour for Distinguished Social Service

Conferred upon GMVS and Secretary Shankar Singh Rawat by the District Collector and District Administration of Ajmer on the occasion of national celebrations, commemorating outstanding grassroots development, girl child education, and women empowerment across rural Ajmer.

“In recognition of unwavering social commitment and impactful transformation of rural marginalized communities across Rajasthan.”
Cultural & Eco-Heritage Honour
International Tribal Culture Festival Committee

Felicitation at International Tribal Culture Festival

Felicitated for exemplary work in reviving indigenous rainwater conservation, safeguarding traditional tribal knowledge, and creating sustainable livelihoods for indigenous communities and pastoral groups in the Aravalli foothills.

“Honouring leadership that revitalizes indigenous eco-wisdom, community soil-water stewardship, and indigenous heritage.”
Social Service Citation
State Dignitaries & Grassroots Apex Forum

Citation for Dedicated Community Service

Presented to GMVS leadership for championing women-led Self-Help Group federations, establishing child-friendly school environments, and ensuring access to primary healthcare and eye care for thousands of elderly rural citizens.

“Exemplary dedication to grassroots community mobilization and building sustainable democratic institutions for rural women.”
🎙️
National Commendation • Mann Ki Baat

Commended by Prime Minister Narendra Modi (Episode 92)

In the 92nd episode of the monthly national broadcast Mann Ki Baat, the Prime Minister commended the rural apparel and women entrepreneurship initiative spearheaded by GMVS Board Member Setha Singh Rawat, highlighting how rural women in Rajasthan are utilizing technology and cluster-based tailoring to access national markets.
