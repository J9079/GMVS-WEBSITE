Institutional Pillars

Our Core Governance Standards

Strong governance is the bedrock of community trust and donor confidence. GMVS maintains institutional systems benchmarked to global NGO accountability standards.

Participatory Social Audits All village project expenditures are presented before the Gram Sabha and Village User Committees for open public review.
Statutory Audits & Public Disclosures Annual financial statements are independently audited by chartered accountants and submitted to the Income Tax Department and Ministry of Home Affairs.
Gender Parity in Institutional Leadership Over 65% of field project personnel, community mobilizers, and committee members are rural women.
