Welcome to GMVS

Developing Women’s Collective & Grassroots Leadership

Gramin Mahila Vikas Sansthan (GMVS) is a non-governmental organization headquartered in Bubani, Ajmer district, Rajasthan.

Operating across rainfall-deficient and arid districts where groundwater depletion, female illiteracy, child marriages, and migration threaten livelihoods, GMVS builds sustainable community institutions. We advocate for local solutions, gender parity, and decentralized community participation.

Empowering Women as Decision Makers Organizing rural women into SHGs, micro-credit clusters, and self-reliant enterprise networks.
Protecting Every Child's Right to Education Rescuing adolescent children from hazardous agricultural labour and re-integrating them into schools.
Restoring Traditional Water Bodies & Soil Reviving historic rainwater harvesting (Taankas, Choukas) to fight severe drought and famine.

Livelihood & Goat Rearing

Goat Rearing Leads to Enhanced Family Income

“Joining the GMVS Self-Help Group gave me the seed capital to buy two goats. Today, I earn a reliable monthly income and my children attend school regularly without fear of debt.”
Rukma Devi, a 40-year-old Jat woman from Khayda village in Bhinai tehsil of Ajmer, was struggling under crushing poverty due to repeated crop failures. GMVS mobilized her into an SHG and linked her to an animal husbandry loan, transforming her from a vulnerable labourer into a self-sufficient livestock entrepreneur.

From Hand-to-Mouth to Full-Time Tailoring Enterprise

“With standard 3 education, I never thought I could run a business. GMVS gave me tailoring training and connected me with local apparel orders. Now I train other village girls.”
Residing at Danta village in Ajmer, Hagami Devi was married young and had limited livelihood avenues. GMVS’s cluster development program provided her with sewing skill training and an affordable sewing machine loan, turning her into a proud village entrepreneur.

Child Rights & Education

Rescued from Child Labour: Untiring Efforts Bear Fruit

“I used to spend 10 hours working in the fields every day instead of studying. GMVS convinced my family to send me back to school. Today I am passing my matriculation with flying colours.”
Ganga, a 15-year-old girl from Bubani village in Ajmer, was forced into manual agricultural labour due to family debt. GMVS field counsellors intervened, provided free textbooks, uniform, and bridge tutoring, enabling Ganga to return to school and pursue her dream of higher education.

National Vision

GMVS and Viksit Bharat 2047

Gramin Mahila Vikas Sansthan is committed to contributing directly to India’s national vision of Viksit Bharat 2047 by empowering rural communities, marginalized groups, women, and youth at the grassroots level.

Through sustainable livelihood promotion, women's economic sovereignty, vocational skill building, adolescent health & wellness, and participatory water management, GMVS ensures that India’s growth is inclusive, equitable, and environmentally resilient.
