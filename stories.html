Stories from the Field

Real testimonies of resilience, human dignity, and sustainable transformation recorded from villages across Ajmer and surrounding districts.

Livelihood & Livestock

Goat Rearing Leads to Enhanced Family Income

“Joining the GMVS Self-Help Group gave me the seed capital to buy two goats. Today, I earn a reliable monthly income and my children attend school regularly without fear of debt.”
Rukma Devi is a 40-year-old Jat woman residing at Khayda village in Bhinai tehsil of Ajmer district. Her husband Hagama Lal was a daily-wage labourer, and recurring monsoon failures pushed the family into acute destitution. GMVS field workers mobilized Rukma Devi into the village SHG, provided financial literacy training, and facilitated an animal husbandry micro-loan. With disciplined care and veterinary support, her herd grew, yielding daily milk and steady market earnings that elevated her family above the poverty line.
RD
Rukma Devi
Khayda Village, Bhinai Tehsil
+₹4,500/mo Income
Women Entrepreneurship

From Hand-to-Mouth to Full-Time Tailoring Enterprise

“With standard 3 education, I never imagined I could run a micro-business. GMVS provided training on a modern sewing machine and linked me with apparel cluster orders.”
Twenty-nine-year-old Hagami Devi is the wife of Shankar Singh and resides at Danta village of Ajmer district. Educated only up to standard 3, Hagami Devi had few economic opportunities. When GMVS launched an apparel skill development workshop in her Panchayat, she enrolled with dedication. Within three months, she mastered cutting, blouse stitching, and school uniform assembly. Today, she earns independent income, contributing significantly to household health and nutrition expenses.
HD
Hagami Devi
Danta Village, Ajmer
Independent Entrepreneur
Child Protection & Education

Untiring Efforts Bear Results: Ganga Returns to Studies

“Working 10 hours a day in the fields left me exhausted and hopeless. GMVS counsellors spoke to my parents repeatedly and enrolled me back in school. Today I am studying for my Class 10 board exams.”
Ganga, aged 15 years, is the daughter of Ram Chandra, residing at Bubani in Ajmer district. Due to her family’s severe debt, she was withdrawn from school to work as an agricultural labourer under harsh sun. GMVS's child rights team intervened through community dialogue, covered her educational expenses, provided bridge study material, and re-enrolled her into the formal school system, freeing her from forced manual labour.
GD
Ganga
Bubani Village, Ajmer
Class 10 Student
Women Empowerment

Dreams of a Bright Future for Chanta Devi

“My husband was ill and our hut had no food grain. Being part of the GMVS collective gave me emergency loan assistance and taught me that poor women do not have to beg.”
Chanta Devi is a Gujjar woman residing at Khayda village in Bhinai tehsil of Ajmer district. Facing severe destitution when her husband fell ill, she joined the local GMVS women's self-help group. Through collective savings and peer solidarity, she secured an emergency medical loan and subsequently invested in small-scale backyard poultry and dairy. Today, she is an active spokesperson in her Panchayat Samiti meetings.
CD
Chanta Devi
Khayda Village, Bhinai
SHG Leader
Child Education & Aspiration

Dreaming to Become a Doctor: Rinku’s Determination

“In our village, girls are married off early. But GMVS gave me the books, science tuition, and courage to tell my family that I want to study science and become a doctor.”
Rinku, aged 13 years, is the daughter of Suva Singh in Muhami village, Ajmer. At age nine, she started working in seasonal cotton and mustard fields to supplement family income. GMVS field educators identified her sharp aptitude, conducted parental awareness sessions, and enrolled her in the village Kishori Mandal. Today, Rinku is consistently ranked top of her class and aspires to serve her community as a medical doctor.
RK
Rinku
Muhami Village, Ajmer
Top Scholar
Tribal Inclusion

Wishing a Bright Future: Guranath from Kalbelia ST Tribe

“As nomadic Kalbelia tribals, my family moved constantly and we had no school papers. GMVS helped me get a birth certificate and an admission into the village school.”
Guranath belongs to the historically marginalized Kalbelia Scheduled Tribe, son of Bannanath. His family is landless and faced extreme social exclusion and lack of basic citizenship documents. GMVS assisted the family with statutory identity papers, provided school bags, uniforms, and remedial Hindi tuition, ensuring Guranath became the first child in his clan to attend regular school.
GN
Guranath
Kalbelia Community, Ajmer
First-Gen Learner
Girl Child Rights

Back to Studies: Chuka Overcomes Neglect

“With 12 brothers and sisters at home, there was never money for my books. GMVS sponsored my education, and now I am learning computer skills.”
Chuka, belonging to a backward caste community, is among 12 siblings of her father Nanu Singh in Muhami village of Ajmer district. Overburdened by domestic chores and childcare, Chuka dropped out in standard 4. GMVS intervened with family counseling, sponsored her complete learning materials, and integrated her into the community digital learning center.

Women Leadership

Empowered Woman: Jhoomi Devi Leads Her Hamlet

“I was once too shy to speak to anyone outside my family. Today, as President of our SHG Federation, I speak directly with bank managers and the District Collector.”
Shy and poor, Jhoomi Devi resided in Nimbukia Ki Dhani under Srinagar Panchayat Samiti of Ajmer district. Through GMVS’s women empowerment training, she learned public speaking, book-keeping, and government scheme navigation. Today, she leads over 150 women across 10 hamlets, resolving local water disputes and facilitating pensions for elderly widows.
JD
Jhoomi Devi
Nimbukia Ki Dhani, Srinagar
Community Federation Leader
Water & Agriculture

Zeal to Do Something: Mira Devi’s Water Revolution

“When our village well dried up, men left for the city. We women joined hands with GMVS to dig an earthen water recharge pond. Today, our wells have water even in May.”
Mira Devi resides in Naulakha village of Srinagar block in Ajmer. With monsoon rains becoming increasingly erratic, village farm production collapsed. Under GMVS guidance, Mira Devi organized village women to construct contour bunds and revive an ancient percolation pond. The renewed water table revived winter mustard crops and stopped seasonal migration.
MD
Mira Devi
Naulakha Village, Srinagar
Water Steward
